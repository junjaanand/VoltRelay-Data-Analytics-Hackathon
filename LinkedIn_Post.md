Day 1 of the Data Analytics Hackathon conducted by Gradient Learning! 🚀

The hackathon has officially kicked off, and I’m thrilled to dive deep into solving a high-stakes, real-world business paradox using data.

For this hackathon, I’m working on **VoltRelay Energy: The Growth-Reliability Paradox**, focused on resolving why an EV battery-swapping network’s rapid growth triggered escalating service failures and unit margin collapse.

Here’s what I’m exploring:
🔹 **The Problem:** VoltRelay tripled completed swaps to 3.6M+ across 6 Indian cities, yet service failures surged past 12% during peak summer, and new-rider 30-day churn jumped from 8% to 23%. Leadership is debating a multi-crore budget: Build more stations? Buy more batteries? Dynamic pricing? Or lock in their biggest fleet customer?

💡 **My Approach:** Using DuckDB and Python to conduct a forensic data audit across 3.88 Million swap records and station telemetry. First step: repairing a tricky firmware v3.2.0 UTC timezone offset bug that distorted 140,000 swap timestamps, and isolating internal test stations.

🔍 **Data & Insights:** 
1. The real bottleneck isn't empty cabinets—it's battery supply! One supplier (Kyron) suffered a catastrophic 100% retirement rate on 2W packs, pulling 1,400+ batteries from the network.
2. In peak summer heat, Gen-1 charging cabinets slowed down by 24% (from 73 to 91 mins), causing 88% of failures due to zero charged batteries.
3. The largest B2B fleet partner's renegotiated 28% discount diluted margins to ₹30.70/swap vs ₹50.04 for retail riders, crowding out high-margin users during dinner rush hours.

🛠 **What I’m Building:** A comprehensive forensic analytics pipeline, interactive diagnostic visuals, and an evidence-backed Capital Budget Decision Model with financial ROI projections.

Today’s biggest learning: Growth without operational unit economics is a trap. Expanding stations when cabinets have no charged batteries just burns capital—the data strongly proves that investing in battery depth and active cabinet cooling delivers a 4.5-month payback and ₹4.86 Cr in recovered margin!
